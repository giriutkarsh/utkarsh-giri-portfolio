02 / ABOUTA LITTLE MORE CONTEXT
UTKARSH GIRI / PROFILE

A journey across creativity, business,
and analytical thinking.

My professional journey began in visual effects. Working across paint, roto-prep and compositing introduced me to structured production pipelines, close attention to detail, quality expectations and collaborative delivery.

Between 2022 and 2024, I continued with freelance VFX work. That period also gave me exposure to independent work and the practical side of managing assignments.

I later pursued an MBA in Business Analytics at COEP Technological University to build my understanding of business processes, data interpretation and decision-making. During my internship at TalentRise Technocrate, I worked on lead evaluation, BRDs, performance reporting and coordination with teams.

I'm now interested in roles across business analysis, data analytics, business intelligence, business operations and creative operations—areas where structured thinking, communication and evidence can help teams make better decisions.
